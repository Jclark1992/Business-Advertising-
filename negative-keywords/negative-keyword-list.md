# Negative Keyword List — Bare Laser & Skin Studio

Status: v1 starter list based on category knowledge, not yet validated against
a real Search Terms report. Apply at account or campaign level as noted; once
you share a Search Terms export, this gets tightened with real wasted-spend
evidence (see `reports/`).

## Account-level negatives (apply everywhere)

**Job/career seekers**
- job, jobs, career, careers, hiring, resume, salary, "how to become"

**DIY / at-home**
- diy, at home, home kit, home use, walmart, amazon, buy laser device,
  "laser hair removal machine", "ipl machine", "for sale"

**Free / cheap intent mismatched with premium service**
- free, "free trial", cheap (keep "affordable"/"cost" — those are fine),
  groupon (unless you're intentionally running Groupon-style promos)

**Wrong service entirely**
- tattoo removal (unless you offer it — confirm), hair transplant,
  laser eye surgery, lasik, laser printer, laser pointer, laser cutting,
  laser engraving, veterinary, pet, dog, cat

**Education/informational only**
- "what is", definition, wikipedia, course, training, certification,
  school, "how does laser hair removal work" (informational-only phrasing;
  keep transactional variants)

**Other locations (if not serving these)**
- calgary, toronto, vancouver, usa, ontario, "bc" — confirm you're Edmonton-only
  before broad-negating other Alberta cities in case you get out-of-town
  clients willing to travel

## Campaign-specific negatives

**Laser Hair Removal campaign**
- ipl (if you split IPL into its own campaign, negative it here to avoid
  overlap/self-competition)
- waxing, sugaring, threading, epilator, shaving (competing modalities
  people research before choosing laser — but consider allowing "laser
  hair removal vs waxing" as it's a comparison searcher, high intent)

**Facials campaign**
- laser, ipl (keep facials campaign clean of hair-removal traffic)
- massage (unless offered)
- makeup, "facial hair" (ambiguous — "facial" + "hair" can trigger for
  men's facial hair removal, review search terms to confirm intent)

**IPL campaign**
- printer, camera, "ipl cricket" (International Premier League — real
  search term collision), "ipl 2024/2025/2026" (cricket league seasons)

## Process

1. This list is the baseline — apply before any campaign goes live.
2. Every week, new search terms from `data/search-term-reports/` get
   reviewed; genuinely irrelevant/wasteful terms get proposed as additions
   here in the weekly report, pending your approval.
3. Never negative a term that's actually converting, even if it looks odd.
